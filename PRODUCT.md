# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Curious players discovering an independent medieval-fantasy voxel sandbox, browsing the world and its development on a desktop or phone. Returning account holders need trustworthy, readable sign-in and security tools without marketing interruptions.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Caelora's world and the freedom to inhabit it. The public site offers exploration and an authentic founding journal, not a storefront. The native Unity game, Astro website, Angular portals and ASP.NET Core identity service remain separate systems. Source: README.md, design-docs/product/website-information-architecture.md and design-docs/product/implementation-roadmap.md, confirmed and extended by the supplied M3.5 briefs.

## Brand Personality

Weathered, expansive, welcoming. Wonder, wilderness, discovery and belonging. A traveller's illustrated atlas, warm stone and timber, distant mountains. Freedom includes a quiet home beside a river. Lore, guilds and eventual player nations are choices, not a compulsory destiny.

## Anti-references

An aggressive MMO sales funnel, neon purple gaming UI, a corporate SaaS dashboard, repeating icon cards, obligatory chosen-one campaigns, fake release dates or invented gameplay imagery. Existing concept studies stay visibly identified as concepts.

## Design Principles

1. Invite the visitor into a world before asking for commitment.
2. Compose around real artwork and truthful development status.
3. Make the scroll journey continuous while preserving natural navigation.
4. Let the visual identity travel between marketing and accounts; keep operational tasks calm.
5. Choose a small, maintainable implementation and measure it in a real browser.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA expectations, visible keyboard focus, semantic navigation, native controls, readable labels, accessible errors, generous touch targets, reflow, static reduced-motion and no-script fallbacks. Automated scores are evidence, not certification. These requirements are explicit in the M3.5 brief and the existing design foundation.
