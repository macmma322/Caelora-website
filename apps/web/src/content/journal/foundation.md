Caelora starts with a world, rather than a destiny assigned to its player. The guiding idea is a medieval-fantasy voxel sandbox where exploration, gathering, survival and building give people room to find their own rhythm.

## A place before a prophecy

We want homes worth returning to, landscapes worth crossing and creatures worth remembering. Villages, guilds and kingdoms belong to that vision. Player-created nations and authoritative multiplayer are longer-term ambitions, not promises of features available today.

## What exists here today

This first public website introduces the project and its art direction. The images are concept artwork, not gameplay footage. They show a visual ambition; they are not a guarantee of final terrain, interfaces, creatures or locations.

The game is a separate native Unity 6 project for Windows. The website is its future commercial and community platform. No release date or price has been announced, and purchases, account registration and launcher downloads are not available here.

## Building the foundations

The first platform milestone establishes a public site and the technical boundaries for future accounts and services. Steam and direct purchases are intended to converge on a single ownership model, but neither purchase route is integrated at this stage.

Future journal entries should document work that can be demonstrated. This note records the direction — and leaves room for development to change it.
